## 1. Front matter (verbatim)

"ODD MOMENTS AND ADDING FRACTIONS / THOMAS F. BLOOM AND VIVIAN KUPERBERG
/ Abstract. We prove near-optimal upper bounds for the odd moments of
the distribution of coprime residues in short intervals, confirming a
conjecture of Montgomery and Vaughan. As an application, we prove
near-optimal upper bounds for the average of the refined singular
series in the Hardy-Littlewood conjectures concerning the number of
prime k-tuples for k odd. The main new ingredient is a near-optimal
upper bound for the number of solutions to $`\sum_{1\le i\le k}
a_i/q_i \in \mathbf{Z}`$ when k is odd, with $`\gcd(a_i,q_i)=1`$ and
restrictions on the size of the numerators and denominators, which is
of independent interest."

## 2. Statement extracted from the anchor (verbatim)

### 2.1 The near-optimal odd-moment bound for the refined singular series (Theorem 2, p.4)

p.4, Theorem 2: "If $`k\ge3`$ is an odd integer and $`h\ge2`$ then

$$R_k(h) \ll (\log h)^{O(1)} h^{\frac{k-1}{2}}.$$

As with Theorem 1, and in keeping with the previous conjecture for the
growth of $`R_k(h)`$, we believe (but cannot prove) that this upper
bound is essentially the best possible, up to the exponent of $`\log
h`$."

Theorem 2 proves a near-optimal (within $`(\log h)^{O(1)}`$) upper bound
for the odd moments $`R_k(h)`$ of the refined singular series, matching
the conjectured power of h.

## 3. Uniformity ledger

Theorem 2 holds for any odd integer $`k\ge3`$ and any $`h\ge2`$; the
implied constant depends on k (per the paper's own footnote convention,
"All implied constants depend (only, yet substantially) on k").
