through the commit. A revert would delete the seven extracts, which are
the only output of the run that stands on primary anchors and not on
report prose, and it would delete the evidence of the incident, which
rule 5 keeps. What has to go is not the file but its standing: a
per-claim verdict register over the prose of a report that no session

single row of it.

preferred discharge; returning the rows to their pre-repair class is

five that carry none of the project's mathematics, and the verdict
class for citations that cannot be anchored at all. Both are operator
decisions carried in the handover decision block since the anchors were
booked.
