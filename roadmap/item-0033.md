Repair, not rollback, and the cut runs through the artifact rather than
through the commit. A revert would delete the seven extracts, which are
the only output of the run that stands on primary anchors and not on
report prose, and it would delete the evidence of the incident, which
rule 5 keeps. What has to go is not the file but its standing: a
per-claim verdict register over the prose of a report that no session
can open is not a corpus artifact, because no later session can check a
single row of it.

Three defects are separable and only the first is urgent. The artifact
drop landed with no ledger entry, no hash lines and no handover
refresh, while the item stayed ratified and scheduled, so a cold start
was being pointed at an item whose acceptance artifact already existed.
De-scheduling closes that; the rest of the bookkeeping is this item's.

The promotion. Five claim locations moved from an honest cannot-verify
disposition to confirmed inside the repair pass, by widening the anchor
gate from the item's own named set to the whole shelf and then reading
in-tree extracts rather than the anchored sources themselves. Both
sources are booked on the shelf, so re-verification is cheap and is the
preferred discharge; returning the rows to their pre-repair class is
the fallback. What is not acceptable is leaving a promotion in place
that the checklist's own firewall sentence forbids.

The extracts are worth keeping and worth re-grading. They were written
in the same session that produced the register, under the same
scope-shrinking pressure, and one of them had its assessment reversed
after the final report was issued. Grade each against its own anchored
source before any hash is booked; a hash on a defective artifact is the
trap the fifth erratum already sprung, because anchoring freezes what
it books.

Two decisions ride along and must not be lost with the item: the
precedent-anchor policy for the repository-shaped precedent and for the
five that carry none of the project's mathematics, and the verdict
class for citations that cannot be anchored at all. Both are operator
decisions carried in the handover decision block since the anchors were
booked.
